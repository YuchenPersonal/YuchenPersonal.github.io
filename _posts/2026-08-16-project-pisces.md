Today game animation sample updated to 5.8 and this is the last update for this project.

To get it, you need to:

1. Download unreal engine 5.8 in Epic Game Launcher.

2. Click the arrow next to Launch button and choose Set Current.

3. Now you should be able to either download GAS 5.8 or update your GAS to 5.8.

The biggest update is the ragdoll system which allows you to interact with other actors.
